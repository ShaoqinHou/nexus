# Workflow mechanism split

Skills are lightweight discovery/reminder entry points, not enforcement. AGENTS.md and .codex/knowledge carry durable guidance; .codex/scripts provide deterministic checks/bookkeeping; Codex hooks are thin triggers that inject state, invalidate gates, or block unsafe commits, but they do not perform review/verification/audit judgment. Review, verification, audit, and pattern promotion remain agent/skill workflows with explicit records. .codex/agents provide isolated roles/model defaults; .codex/dashboard visualizes records for humans and future agents.
